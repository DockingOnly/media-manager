Settings

Get a free key at themoviedb.org → Settings → API.

Subtitles (OpenSubtitles.com)

Create a free account at opensubtitles.com, then get an API key under your profile → API consumers. The username and password are optional: without them the key's own daily allowance is used, and with them your account's. Each run tells you how many downloads are left today. Languages are two-letter codes, separated by commas.

YouTube login (for age-restricted trailers)

Some trailers are age-restricted, and YouTube only shows them to signed-in users. The app tries every trailer without your login first and only uses it for those. Be signed in to youtube.com in the browser you choose. Firefox works directly. Chrome and Edge usually lock their logins on Windows; with those, install a “cookies.txt” export extension (for example “Get cookies.txt LOCALLY”), export while on youtube.com, and choose “A cookies.txt file”. Your login never leaves this computer.

Ratings (OMDb, optional)

Adds IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic ratings to the .nfo files the app writes (IMDb becomes the rating Jellyfin shows; Rotten Tomatoes its critic rating). Get a free key (1,000 lookups a day) at omdbapi.com, and activate it from the email OMDb sends.

Rename patterns

How the renamer names folders and files. Placeholders in curly brackets are filled in; empty ones (a missing year, say) are tidied away. Season 0 is always “Specials”, and episode names must include {code} (the S01E02 part Jellyfin reads).

Artwork (fanart.tv, optional)

Posters, backdrops and logos come from TMDB and need nothing more. A free fanart.tv key adds banners, clear art, disc art and landscape thumbnails, and more choices for the rest. Create an account at fanart.tv, then get a personal API key from your profile.

Your keys and password are stored only in config.json on this computer.

Enter the folder that holds your movies, then choose Scan folder. Next time, your library opens automatically.

    Working out the changes…

    Write .nfo files

    Writes a Kodi/Jellyfin .nfo next to each matched movie, with title, plot, cast, genres and TMDB/IMDb IDs. Movies that still need review are left out. Rename first so the files get the right names.

    Updating keeps everything else in them, such as watched status, play counts, tags and ratings from tinyMediaManager, and your own edits.

    Download artwork

    Fills in missing artwork for every matched movie, with the names Jellyfin looks for (poster.jpg, fanart.jpg, clearlogo.png, landscape.jpg, banner.jpg, clearart.png, disc.png; or Movie (Year)-clearlogo.png and so on when a movie shares its folder). Artwork you already have is recognised and kept. To pick a different image for one movie, open it and use its Artwork section.

    Banners, clear art and disc art come from fanart.tv: add a free key in Settings to get them.

    Choose

    The image you pick replaces the current one, which goes to the Recycle Bin.

    Loading…

    Saved straight into the movie's .nfo, keeping everything else in it. Jellyfin is told not to overwrite your edits, and the app keeps them when it updates .nfo files. A new title or year also becomes the name the renamer uses.

    Edit selected

    Each title's .nfo is updated (everything else in it is kept) and locked so Jellyfin keeps the change. Titles without a confirmed match are skipped.

    Collections

      Jellyfin builds these collections from the .nfo files (with “Automatically add to collection” on in its library settings) and fetches their artwork itself.

      Loading…

      Saved into tvshow.nfo, keeping everything else in it, and locked so Jellyfin keeps your edits. A new title or year also becomes the show folder's name for the renamer.

      Delete

      Download subtitles

      Checks your folder for changes, then searches OpenSubtitles.com for each matched movie or episode. Subtitles made for your exact copy of the file are picked first, so they're in sync. They're saved the way Jellyfin expects, for example Heat (1995).en.srt, or Heat (1995).en.sdh.srt for hearing-impaired versions.

      Movies that already have a subtitle file in a language are skipped for that language. OpenSubtitles limits downloads per day (about 20 on a free account); when the limit is reached the app stops and tells you when it resets.

      Download trailers

      First checks your folder for changes, then for each matched movie (or TV show):

      • keeps a trailer it already has, renaming or moving it to the right Jellyfin name if the movie was renamed;
      • replaces a trailer that belongs to a different movie (for example after you changed the match), sending the old one to the Recycle Bin;
      • downloads the official trailer from YouTube when there is none.

      Trailers go in a trailers folder inside the movie's (or show's) folder, or as Movie (Year)-trailer.mkv next to the movie when it shares a folder.

      Downloads run one at a time, so a large library can take a while. You can cancel from the progress bar, and anything already downloaded is kept.
